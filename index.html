How each course works

Every module opens with a clinical scenario, explains the basics, and gives you an interactive model to change. Each ends with a short experiment and a quiz. Each course also has a graded challenge, a clinical case library and homework. Everything runs in your browser; nothing to install.

Simplified models and synthetic data for teaching only, not for clinical use.
